A legacy of orthopaedic care

Built in Nagpur. Focused on better mobility.

Dr H J Sangtani returned to Nagpur after years of orthopaedic work and training in the UK and established Ortho Relief Hospital & Research Centre in 1992 as a 30-bed facility. The hospital has since expanded its infrastructure and clinical scope.

The current public hospital website describes a 2021 expansion to 50 beds with two operating theatres, a CathLab, ICU, CT scan, expanded OPD and IPD amenities.

Selected milestones

A story worth showing.

1992

Ortho Relief Hospital & Research Centre established as a 30-bed facility, according to the hospital's public website.

1993

Hospital website states Dr H J Sangtani performed Total Knee Replacement Surgery in Central India.

2005

Hospital website highlights a Central India first for hip-resurfacing surgery.

2006

Hospital website records its first Computer Aided Surgery for Joint Replacement and acquisition of CT-free navigation technology.

2009

Hospital website records a Total Elbow Joint Replacement milestone in Central India.

2021

New wing expanded the stated capacity to 50 beds and added two OTs, CathLab, ICU, CT scan and expanded OPD/IPD facilities.
